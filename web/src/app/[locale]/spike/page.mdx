This throwaway page proves the whole content chain works before any real
content exists. If you can read the equation below with proper math
typesetting, build-time KaTeX works.

Attention scores are scaled by $\sqrt{d_k}$ before the softmax:

$$
\mathrm{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
$$

<Junior>
  **Junior view.** Big dot products make softmax too confident — dividing by
  $\sqrt{d_k}$ keeps the scores in a sane range.
</Junior>

<Senior>
  **Senior view.** If $q, k \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ i.i.d., then
  $\mathrm{Var}(q \cdot k) = d_k$; dividing by $\sqrt{d_k}$ restores unit
  variance, keeping softmax out of its vanishing-gradient saturation regime
  at initialization.
</Senior>
