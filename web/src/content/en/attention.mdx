# Self-attention

Every position asks a question ($Q$), every position advertises what it has
($K$), and information flows where they agree:

$$
\mathrm{Attention}(Q,K,V) = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top + M}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
$$

<Junior>
  Read one row of the softmax matrix: it's that character deciding **which
  earlier characters to look at**, with weights that sum to 1. The dark upper
  triangle is the causal mask — you can't look at the future. Flip between the
  six heads: each one learned a different habit (previous character, start of
  the word, matching punctuation…).
</Junior>

<Senior>
  With $q, k$ roughly unit-variance, $\mathrm{Var}(q \cdot k) = d_k$ — so raw
  scores at $d_k = 32$ have σ ≈ 5.7, enough to saturate softmax and kill
  gradients at init. Dividing by $\sqrt{d_k}$ restores unit variance; compare
  the first two heatmaps and watch the color range shrink.

  The mask adds $-\infty$ *before* softmax, so masked positions get exactly
  $e^{-\infty} = 0$ probability — and exactly zero gradient (see the backward
  mirror). Heads are just a reshape: the same $[T, 192]$ qkv tensor viewed as
  6 independent $[T, 32]$ subspaces — no extra parameters, no extra FLOPs
  versus one big head, but 6 separate attention strategies.
</Senior>
