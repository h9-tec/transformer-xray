# MLP

Widen ×4, gate, project back:

$$
\mathrm{MLP}(x) = \mathrm{GELU}(xW_1 + b_1)\,W_2 + b_2
$$

<Junior>
  Attention *moves* information between positions; the MLP *thinks* about each
  position independently. It expands every 192-dim vector to 768 numbers,
  keeps the useful ones (GELU lets positives through and squashes negatives),
  and compresses back. Look at the gelu heatmap: the blue half of fc has gone
  dark.
</Junior>

<Senior>
  GELU here is the **tanh approximation** — the exact GPT-2 formula:

  $$
  0.5x\left(1 + \tanh\!\left[\sqrt{2/\pi}\,(x + 0.044715x^3)\right]\right)
  $$

  Never mix it with the erf form: they differ by ~10⁻³ and cross-implementation
  parity dies. Unlike ReLU it's smooth (no dead gradients at 0⁻), and its
  derivative at 0 is exactly ½.

  The two matmuls here are ⅔ of the block's FLOPs — $2 \cdot 4d^2$ each vs
  $4d^2$ for all of attention's projections — which is why MLP kernels
  dominate GPU time in real models too.
</Senior>
